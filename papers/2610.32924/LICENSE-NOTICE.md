# Source and graphics

Maxime Chambonneau, Mario Garcia-Lechuga, David Grojo, and Stefan Nolte, *Scaling the Wavelength to Optimize Ultrafast Laser Processing of Bandgap Materials*, [arXiv:2610.32924v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32924v1). The arXiv record lists [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). This adaptation transcribes and repositions text and captions from the preprint PDF.

The preprint incorporates third-party figures with separate reuse terms. The extracted page graphics are omitted from the public adaptation, including graphical formula fragments on pages that use them. Consult the corresponding pages of the arXiv PDF for the complete display. Local copies of the extracted page assets can be enabled by adding `--input original-graphics=true` to the Typst compile command.

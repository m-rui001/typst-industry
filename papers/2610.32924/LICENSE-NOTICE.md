# Source and graphics

Maxime Chambonneau, Mario Garcia-Lechuga, David Grojo, and Stefan Nolte, *Scaling the Wavelength to Optimize Ultrafast Laser Processing of Bandgap Materials*, [arXiv:2610.32924v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32924v1). The arXiv record lists [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). This adaptation transcribes and repositions text and captions from the preprint PDF.

The published page graphics restore formula fragments and Figures 14.1, 14.4, 14.12–14.14, 14.16, 14.18, and 14.20–14.21. The source credits Figures 14.1 and 14.4 to chapter coauthors. The other included figures are identified as CC BY 4.0 in their captions, which retain the individual credits and citations.

Other figures include third-party panels reproduced by permission. Consult the [source PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.32924v1) for those figures. With local copies of all extracted page assets in the graphics directory, the full set can be enabled by adding `--input original-graphics=true` to the Typst command.
